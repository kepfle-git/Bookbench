# Code of conduct

Bookbench is a small project maintained in spare time. Everyone who takes part - in issues, pull
requests or discussions - is welcome, whatever their background or experience.

- Be respectful and assume good intentions. Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive; bug reports with steps to reproduce help most.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualised language or publishing other people's private
  information.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules and may
block people who keep breaking them. Problems can be reported privately to the maintainer through
GitHub ([kepfle-git](https://github.com/kepfle-git)).

Inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
